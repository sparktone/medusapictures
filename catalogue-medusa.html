1980s – 2000s — United Kingdom

Medusa Pictures

The original British home-video label, active from the 1980s to the 2000s. Its catalogue spans action, horror, thriller, and international genre releases distributed on VHS across the United Kingdom, as well as theatrical releases ranging from the cult classic Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles to Rob Reiner's legendary film adaptation of Stephen King's Misery.
They have been mainly known as Medusa Communications Limited, and later as Medusa Communications and Marketing Limited, but often branded as Medusa Pictures. For clarity, we will mainly refer to them as the latter.
